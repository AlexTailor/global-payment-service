1Global Payment Service — single-page app, mobile first

Built on Nocturne: dark ground, outlined primary actions, Inter at 500, 8px radii, rules that fade at their ends. One page — header with account switcher, balance, transaction list, everything else in sheets over it. Phone frames are 390×844; the tablet and desktop frames show the same screen adapted up. Status colour: COMPLETED takes the accent tint, PROCESSING an accent outline, FAILED a red held at the accent's own lightness and chroma (oklch 0.66 / 0.125, hue 25) — the one colour added outside the mono palette, because a payments list needs failure to read at a glance.
